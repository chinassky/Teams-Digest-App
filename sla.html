Support & Service Level Statement

Last updated: 11/08/2026

How to reach us

For any question, bug report, or feature request related to "Teams Digest for Jira", contact us at: support@teamsdigestforjira.com.

Response time commitment

SeverityDescriptionTarget first response
CriticalThe app is completely unable to deliver digests for a paying customerWithin 1 business day
StandardA feature isn't working as expected, but a workaround existsWithin 2 business days
GeneralQuestions, feature requests, or minor issuesWithin 3 business days

Business hours

Support is provided by a small, independent team. We aim to respond within the windows above during business days (Monday–Friday, excluding public holidays in Mexico City CST).

Scope

This statement covers support for the App's configuration, scheduling, and delivery to Microsoft Teams. It does not cover issues originating from Microsoft Teams, Power Automate, or your own Jira instance's configuration outside of the App.
